## TEMPLATE 1 — Supplier Directory Listing Request

**Use for:** TradeWheel, ExportHub, Maker's Row, Wonnda, Baramdat (directory listing)

**Subject line variations (A/B test):**
1. Pakistan leather manufacturer — directory listing request
2. New supplier profile submission — leather goods & hides
3. Leather OEM manufacturer seeking listing on [DIRECTORY NAME]

**Email body (under 120 words):**

> Hi [NAME or "team"],
>
> I'm Ameer from Pure Grain Exports, a leather manufacturer based in Pakistan exporting finished hides (cow, buffalo, goat) and OEM leather goods (wallets, belts, bags, jackets) to buyers in the US, UK, Canada, and UAE.
>
> I'd like to create a supplier profile on [DIRECTORY NAME]. We serve private-label brands with MOQs starting at [MOQ] units and handle everything from sampling through FBA-ready packaging.
>
> I've attached our company one-pager with certifications, product photos, and export references. Happy to provide whatever format your directory requires.
>
> What's the best way to get listed?
>
> Best,
> Ameer — Pure Grain Exports
> [WEBSITE] | [PHONE]

**Common pitfalls to avoid:**
- Don't write a wall of text — directory admins review hundreds of submissions
- Don't oversell — let the one-pager do the heavy lifting
- Don't forget to include the one-pager as an attachment

**What to attach/link:**
- Company one-pager PDF (1 page: capabilities, MOQ, certifications, 4–6 product photos, export markets)
- Link to puregrainexports.com
- 2–3 high-resolution product photos (separate files or hosted links)

## TEMPLATE 2 — Guest Article Pitch

**Use for:** B2B Headlines, Wonnda Magazine, industry blogs

**Subject line variations (A/B test):**
1. Article pitch: Why brands are shifting leather sourcing from China to Pakistan
2. Guest contribution idea — Pakistan leather sourcing data for [PUBLICATION]
3. Pitch: The real cost breakdown of OEM leather goods from South Asia

**Email body (under 150 words):**

> Hi [EDITOR NAME],
>
> I read your piece on [SPECIFIC ARTICLE TITLE] — your breakdown of [SPECIFIC DETAIL FROM ARTICLE] was especially useful.
>
> I'd like to propose a guest article for [PUBLICATION NAME]:
>
> **"[PROPOSED TITLE — e.g., 'Pakistan vs. China for Leather Sourcing: A 2026 Cost & Quality Comparison']"**
>
> The article would cover actual per-unit manufacturing costs, MOQ structures, lead times, and compliance data from our factory floor — not generic sourcing advice but supplier-side numbers that buyers rarely see published.
>
> Quick background: Pure Grain Exports is a Pakistan-based OEM leather manufacturer supplying private-label brands in the US, UK, and EU. We produce finished hides and leather goods (wallets, belts, bags, jackets).
>
> Would this fit your editorial calendar? Happy to send an outline.
>
> Best,
> Ameer — Pure Grain Exports

**Common pitfalls to avoid:**
- Never send a generic pitch — always reference a specific article they published
- Don't propose a topic that's a thinly veiled product advertisement
- Don't send the full article unsolicited — pitch the idea first, then write on acceptance

**What to attach/link:**
- Nothing on first email — keep it lightweight
- If they reply with interest: send a 5-bullet outline + link to any published Pure Grain content

## TEMPLATE 5 — Follow-Up Email (7 days after no response)

**Subject line:** Re: [ORIGINAL SUBJECT LINE]

**Email body (under 60 words):**

> Hi [NAME],
>
> Just bumping my note from last week about [SPECIFIC TOPIC — e.g., "listing Pure Grain on your directory" or "the Pakistan leather sourcing article idea"]. Totally understand if the timing isn't right.
>
> Would it help if I sent a quick company one-pager?
>
> Best,
> Ameer

**Common pitfalls to avoid:**
- Never follow up more than twice total (initial + 2 follow-ups max)
- Don't add new information or change the ask — keep it about the original pitch
- Don't apologize excessively — one polite bump is professional, not pushy

**What to attach/link:**
- Nothing — keep it minimal. Attach only if they specifically ask.
