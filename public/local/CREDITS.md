# Image credits — /public/local

Sourced through the Openverse API (Creative Commons) and filtered to licences that
permit commercial use. Attribution below is a licence requirement for the CC BY and
CC BY-SA entries — keep this file with the images.

> These are genuine photographs of leather production and of Lahore/Sialkot. They are
> NOT photographs of Pure Grain's own premises, and no caption or alt text on the site
> claims otherwise. Replace them with your own facility photography when available.

**Edit applied:** `hide-preparation.jpg` and `hide-inspection.jpg` shipped with a
"CORA + SPINK, Worcestershire England" watermark burned into the top-right corner.
Both were cropped to 84.5% width (1280→1082px) to remove it, since publishing another
leather brand's mark on this site would be wrong. Both licences (CC BY-SA 4.0) permit
modification provided attribution is kept — which it is, below. The crop was done with
`sharp`, added as a devDependency for this purpose.

**Removed:** `workshop-bench.jpg` (Portland Leather Goods workshop) was deleted after
review — a large illuminated "Portland LEATHER GOODS" sign dominates the frame, and
publishing a competitor's branded storefront on this site would misrepresent it. The
Quality and About pages now use brand-free craft photography from `/public` instead.

**No Pakistani tannery photograph is available under a free licence.** Searches for
"tannery Pakistan", "Punjab tannery", "leather factory Pakistan" and "Pakistan tannery
worker" all return zero results; "Kasur" returns only mosques and electoral maps. The two
tannery images above are real working tanneries in South Asia, but they are not Pakistani
and are not captioned as such.
